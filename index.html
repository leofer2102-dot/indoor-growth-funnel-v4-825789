You don't need better instincts. You need the right system to replace four common—and costly—beginner myths.

Myth: “Plants need constant watering”

Reality: Overwatering is the #1 cause of plant death. Roots suffocate without oxygen.

Reality: Scorched leaves and light stress ruin growth; bright indirect light is the true sweet spot.

Reality: Yellowing is usually root distress or moisture imbalance.
